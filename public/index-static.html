About us

The most private room in the home deserves the most thought.

東洋陶器 Tōyō Tōki, TOTO's founding name. Kitakyushu, Japan, 1917.

For more than a century TOTO has studied one space with unusual devotion. The result is a bathroom that helps keep itself clean, warms to your arrival and uses less water, without asking anything of you. Engineered in Japan, made in India.

The technology inside

Explore our technology
  1. 01

    CEFIONTECT

    An ultra-smooth, fired glaze that leaves waste almost nothing to cling to.

  2. 02

    TORNADO FLUSH

    Twin nozzles create a powerful swirling rinse that cleans the whole bowl, quietly and with less water.

  3. 03

    EWATER+

    Electrolyzed water cleans the wand and bowl after use, with no chemicals or detergents.

  4. 04

    ACTILIGHT

    A special glaze and a built-in clean light in the lid work together, so waste washes away more easily.
